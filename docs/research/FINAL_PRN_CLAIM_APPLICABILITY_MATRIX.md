# FINAL PRN claim applicability matrix

| Claim class | Allowed? | Surface | Limitation |
|-------------|----------|---------|------------|
| Supports reason-and-result PRN charting | Yes | product docs | Not certified MAR |
| Designed around medication-safety guidance | Yes | internal/docs | Multi-source; setting-scoped |
| Auditable administration history | Yes | audit/history | Not eMAR certification |
| Configurable policy packs | Yes (partial) | architecture | Full packs incomplete |
| Human verification required | Yes | Relay confirm | Always |
| Government / CMS / JC / FDA certified | **No** | all | Forbidden without formal review |
| HIPAA compliant (blanket) | **No** | all | Forbidden without qualified review |
| Universally legally sufficient | **No** | all | Forbidden |

**System safety behavior** is software-testable.  
**Organization policy** is configurable.  
**Jurisdiction / individual authority to administer** requires qualified human review and remains outside automated certification claims.
