# FINAL PRN external authority limitations

1. **California DDS** materials apply to covered developmental services / vendor and training contexts. They do not automatically govern every family home or every US state.

2. **CMS** documentation references used in research are context-bound (e.g. LTC / Part D validation themes). They do not certify Caretaker Relay as a CMS-compliant eMAR.

3. **DailyMed / RxNorm** support medication identity and labeling references. They must never override a recipient’s authorized order or invent a dose.

4. **ACL** criteria guide product evaluation for caregiver AI (burden, usability, safety, collaboration). They are not medication-administration statutes.

5. **Individual caregiver authority** to administer or assist with PRN medication is role-, setting-, and order-dependent and is **not** established by a software Seven Rights checklist alone.
