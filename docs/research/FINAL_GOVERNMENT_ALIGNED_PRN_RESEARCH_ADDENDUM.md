## California DDS — Seven Rights & PRN MAR

Emphasized practices (training / vendor materials): right person, medication, dose, time, route, reason, documentation; PRN entries include reason and result; communicate what was / was not given at transitions.

**Relay mapping:** episode fields + handoff open line + cross-shift continuity.

**Caveat:** CA developmental services / community settings — not automatic law for every family home or other state.

## CMS documentation themes (LTC / Part D context materials)

Active medication orders should identify medication instructions and authorization by a person with prescriptive authority in applicable contexts.

**Relay mapping:** authorized `PrnOrder` separate from caregiver-reported episode and unauthorized OTC report.

**Caveat:** Setting-specific CMS materials — not a claim of CMS certification.

## Product boundary

Relay is a **protocol engine** for multi-role care spaces. Claims allowed only per `COMPLIANCE_CLAIM_REGISTRY.md`.
