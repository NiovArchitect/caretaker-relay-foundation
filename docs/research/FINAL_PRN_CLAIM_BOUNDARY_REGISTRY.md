# FINAL PRN CLAIM BOUNDARY REGISTRY

| Claim | Allowed | Limitation |
|-------|---------|------------|
| Supports reason-and-result PRN charting | Yes | Not certified MAR |
| Designed around medication-safety guidance | Yes | Multi-source; setting-scoped |
| Auditable administration history | Yes | Not eMAR certification |
| Human verification required | Yes | Always |
| Configurable organization/jurisdiction policy | Yes (partial packs) | Full packs incomplete |
| Government / CMS / FDA / JC certified | **Forbidden** | — |
| Certified MAR / eMAR | **Forbidden** | — |
| HIPAA compliant (blanket) | **Forbidden** without qualified review | — |
| Universally legally sufficient | **Forbidden** | — |
| ACL approval of clinical effectiveness | **Forbidden** | ACL judging is not that determination |

System safety is software-testable. Organization policy is configurable. Jurisdiction and individual authority to administer require qualified human review.
